TwinlyPrivacy policy

Delete your Twinly account

You can request deletion of your Twinly account and associated app data at any time.

Delete in the app

Open Twinly and go to Settings → Account → Delete account. Follow the confirmation steps. Firebase may ask you to re-enter your password before deletion can be completed.

Request deletion by email

Email sushdevv05@gmail.com with:

  • Subject: Twinly Account Deletion Request
  • The email address associated with your Twinly account

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • Your UID-scoped Twinly server profile
  • Local saved looks, fashion profiles, avatar data, and usage data cleared by the app
External provider notice: Data held by third-party image providers may not be immediately deletable if those providers do not expose deletion controls. Twinly does not control those providers' retention policies.

Processing time

Email deletion requests are processed within 30 days. In-app deletion begins immediately, subject to authentication and provider limitations.

Contact

For questions, contact sushdevv05@gmail.com.
